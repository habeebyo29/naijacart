Shipping & Delivery

We work to get your tech products to you safely and conveniently. Here's what you need to know about shipping and delivery at NaijaCart.

Delivery Areas

We deliver to locations covered by our available delivery services. Delivery availability may depend on your location.

Order Processing

After your order is confirmed, we prepare your items for dispatch. Processing time may vary depending on the product and order details.

Delivery Time

Delivery time can vary depending on your location, delivery availability, and other factors. We'll provide relevant delivery information when available.

Safe Delivery

We take care when preparing orders for delivery. Please check your package and items when your order arrives.

How Delivery Works

Your order goes through a simple delivery process.

1

Place Your Order

Choose your products and complete your order through NaijaCart.

2

Order Confirmation

Your order is received and prepared for processing.

3

Dispatch

Once your order is ready, it is handed over for delivery.

4

Delivery

Your order is delivered to the address provided during checkout.

Keep Your Contact Details Updated

Make sure the phone number and delivery information provided with your order are correct so our delivery team can reach you when necessary.

Helpful Delivery Tips

  • Double-check your delivery address before completing your order.
  • Keep your phone available when your order is expected.
  • Check your package when it arrives.
  • Contact our support team if you have questions about your delivery.

Need help with delivery?

Our support team is available to help with questions about your order and delivery.
