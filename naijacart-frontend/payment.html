Payment Methods

Choose a convenient payment option when completing your NaijaCart order. We aim to make checkout simple, convenient, and secure.

Debit & Credit Cards

Pay for your order using an eligible debit or credit card through the available payment options at checkout.

Bank Transfer

Where available, you may be able to complete your payment through a supported bank transfer option.

Online Payment

Available online payment options will be displayed during checkout based on the payment services supported by NaijaCart.

Payment Confirmation

After a successful payment, your order will proceed according to the checkout and order processing process.

How Payment Works

Completing your payment is simple.

1

Add Your Products

Select the products you want and add them to your cart.

2

Continue to Checkout

Review your order and provide the required delivery information.

3

Choose a Payment Method

Select one of the payment options available during checkout.

4

Complete Your Payment

Follow the payment instructions provided and complete your order.

Keep Your Payment Information Safe

Never share your card PIN, password, verification codes, or other confidential payment information with anyone.

Helpful Payment Tips

  • Check your order total before making payment.
  • Make sure your payment details are entered correctly.
  • Keep your payment confirmation or order information for your records.
  • Contact NaijaCart support if you have a payment-related question.
