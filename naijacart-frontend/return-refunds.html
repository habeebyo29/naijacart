Returns & Refunds

We want you to shop with confidence. If something isn't right with your order, here's what you need to know about returns and refunds.

Return Policy

If you receive an item that is damaged, defective, incorrect, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible so we can help resolve the issue.

Eligible Items

Items should generally be returned in their original condition, with their packaging and accessories where applicable.

Refunds

Once an approved return has been reviewed, we'll process the appropriate refund according to the original order and payment arrangement.

Items Not Eligible

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their condition, usage, or product-specific requirements.

How to Request a Return

Follow these simple steps if you need assistance with a return.

1

Contact Us

Get in touch with our support team and provide your order details.

2

Explain the Issue

Tell us what went wrong and, where necessary, provide relevant information about the item.

3

Return Instructions

Our team will let you know the next steps if your return request is approved.

4

Refund Processing

After the return is reviewed, any approved refund will be processed accordingly.

Refund Processing

Refund timing can vary depending on the return review and the payment method used for the order. Our team will provide any relevant information during the process.

Before Returning an Item

Please keep your order information and product packaging available until your return has been resolved. Do not send an item back without first contacting NaijaCart for return instructions.

Need help with a return?

Our support team is ready to help you with questions about your order.
