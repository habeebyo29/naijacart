NAIJACART PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information NaijaCart may collect, how it may be used, and the steps we take to protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use NaijaCart, we may collect information that you provide when creating an account, placing an order, or contacting our support team.

  • Name and contact information.
  • Delivery and order information.
  • Account information.
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through NaijaCart may be used to:

  • Process and manage your orders.
  • Arrange delivery of purchased products.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain and improve our website.
  • Communicate with you about your orders.

3. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through supported payment services. Payment information may be handled by the relevant payment provider according to their own privacy and security policies.

NaijaCart does not ask customers to send confidential payment credentials such as card PINs or verification codes through messages or customer support.

4. Delivery Information

We may use the delivery information you provide to process and deliver your orders.

This may include information such as your delivery address and contact details needed to complete the delivery.

5. Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled through NaijaCart against unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security, so customers should also take care to protect their account details.

6. Account Security

If you have a NaijaCart account, keep your password confidential and avoid sharing your login information with others.

If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, contact us as soon as possible.

7. Sharing Information

We may share information when necessary to provide services such as order processing, payment processing, delivery, customer support, or other functions required to operate NaijaCart.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

8. Cookies & Website Data

NaijaCart may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to help the website function properly and provide a better shopping experience.

Your browser may allow you to manage cookie settings.

9. Third-Party Services

NaijaCart may use third-party services such as payment providers, delivery services, hosting providers, or other technologies needed to operate the website.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as NaijaCart develops or when changes are required.

Updated information will be made available on this page.

Your Privacy Matters

Please avoid sharing passwords, card PINs, verification codes, or other confidential information with anyone claiming to represent NaijaCart.

Have a Privacy Question?

If you have questions about how your information is handled, please contact our support team.
