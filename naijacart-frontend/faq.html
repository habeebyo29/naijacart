NAIJACART SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about shopping, orders, payments, delivery, returns, and refunds on NaijaCart.

Shopping & Orders

How do I place an order?
Browse our products, add the items you want to your cart, review your order, and continue to checkout to complete your purchase.
Can I order more than one product?
Yes. You can add multiple products to your cart and review everything together before completing your order.
Can I cancel my order?
If you need to cancel an order, contact our support team as soon as possible. Cancellation availability may depend on the order's current status.

Payments

What payment methods are available?
Available payment methods are displayed during checkout. Depending on the available payment services, options may include card and bank-based payments.
Is my payment information safe?
Keep your payment credentials private and only enter payment information through the official checkout process. Never share your PIN or verification codes with anyone.
What should I do if my payment fails?
Check your payment details and try again. If the problem continues, contact our support team for assistance.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?
Delivery time depends on your location, product availability, and the delivery service handling your order.
Can I track my order?
You can visit our Track Order page for information about how order delivery works. Delivery information will be provided when available.
What if I miss my delivery?
If you miss a delivery attempt, follow any instructions provided by the delivery service or contact NaijaCart support for assistance.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return an item?
Items may be eligible for return depending on the condition of the product and the circumstances of the order. Please contact our support team before returning an item.
How do refunds work?
Once an approved return has been reviewed, any applicable refund will be processed according to the order and payment method.
What if I receive a damaged item?
Contact our support team as soon as possible and provide your order information so we can help you with the next steps.

Still Have Questions?

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, our support team is ready to help.
